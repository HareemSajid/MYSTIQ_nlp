The field guide

How to play

You are a consulting detective in Victorian London. Each case is a bundle of documents: statements, letters, registers, diaries, newspaper reports and schedules.

{% icon "board" %}
01 · CASE BOARD

Read the briefing

Read the introduction and the objective, and meet the persons of interest.

{% icon "ner" %}
02 · EVIDENCE

Open every document

The viewer marks the people, places, objects and times in the text, and lets you question the files in plain English. Answers cite their sources.

{% icon "stance" %}
03 · STATEMENTS

How does each witness know?

Marked as observation, assumption, hearsay or denial. “Why is this suspicious?” compares it with every record — a contradiction needs matching people, day, place and time.

{% icon "time" %}
04 · TIMELINE

Watch the day unfold

Built automatically from every time found in every document. Records and claims are drawn differently.

{% icon "chat" %}
05 · INTERROGATION

Question the suspects

They stay in character and never change their story — but they may evade, mislead or answer only literally.

{% icon "lamp" %}
06 · DR. WATSON

Ask — or take a hint

He explains evidence and nudges your reasoning. Exactly three hints exist per case, each costs points.

{% icon "gavel" %}
07 · FINAL DEDUCTION

Prove it

Name the culprit, what happened, when, the evidence, and the contradiction or hidden meaning that exposed the truth — then explain it in your own words. Solve a case to unlock the next.

Scoring

Max 1000 points

Hints cost 75 each, extra attempts 40 each, and slow cases lose points after ten minutes.
